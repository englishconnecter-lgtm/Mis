Briefing: Recover the Lost Briefing

You are a language analyst at ESL CONNECT Intelligence. A confidential briefing has been scrambled. To reconstruct it, you must unlock five mission rooms by using advanced phrasal verbs accurately.
How to play
Complete each mission. Correct answers give points and build your streak. Wrong answers show red feedback, but you can try again. Your rank changes as your score increases.

Boss Level: Create the Briefing

Use at least six target phrasal verbs to write a short intelligence-style briefing.

Challenge verbs: look into, carry out, put forward, run up against, get across, bring about, cut back on, make up for, turn down, fall through.

Model answer:
The committee carried out a review after it came across several inconsistencies. Investigators looked into the issue and put forward three possible explanations. However, the team ran up against resistance from senior managers, so the final report struggled to get across the seriousness of the problem. New measures were introduced to cut back on unnecessary spending and make up for previous losses.
