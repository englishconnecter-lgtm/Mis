40-question English level entry test

This multiple-choice test starts with easier items and becomes more difficult as you move forward. At the end, the page estimates a CEFR level from A1 to C2 based on total score and performance across the increasing difficulty bands.

Format:
40 multiple-choice questions
Progression:
A1-A2, then B1-B2, then C1-C2
Result:
Instant CEFR estimate

Difficulty bands

Questions 1-10
Basic language use, everyday grammar, common vocabulary.
Questions 11-20
Elementary to lower-intermediate grammar and reading.
Questions 21-30
Independent user tasks, more precise structure and meaning.
Questions 31-40
Advanced control, nuance, and formal language use.
